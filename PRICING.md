# Pricing tiers (draft)

All figures are **USD, excluding tax and cloud/model consumption** (Azure, OpenAI/Foundry usage is billed at cost or to the client's own subscription). These are opening proposals built on an assumed senior blended rate of about $200-225/hr; validate against your target margin, region and market before publishing.

## Pricing principles

- Fixed price wherever scope can be bounded (assessment, PoC, migration); time-and-materials only for open-ended build work.
- Every project engagement starts with a paid assessment or PoC. Its fee is credited against the follow-on project if signed within 30 days.
- Retainers are monthly, 3-month minimum, unused hours roll over one month.
- Rate card for anything outside a tier: Principal $275/hr, Senior engineer $200/hr, Engineer $150/hr.
