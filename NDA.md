Each is a "**party**" and together the "**parties**". A party disclosing information is the "**Discloser**" and a party receiving it is the "**Recipient**".

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to discuss and evaluate a potential business relationship concerning {{PURPOSE, e.g. "an assessment and possible migration of the Counterparty's prototype application to production, and related AI integration services"}} (the "**Purpose**"). Each may disclose Confidential Information to the other for the Purpose only.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, disclosed by or for the Discloser (or its Affiliates) to the Recipient in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the Effective Date, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances. It includes, without limitation:

- source code, repositories, prompts, models, datasets, architectures, schemas, credentials and security information;
- business plans, financial data, pricing, customer and supplier information; and
- the existence and content of the parties' discussions, the Purpose, and any proposals exchanged.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show: (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to it without confidentiality duty before disclosure; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) was independently developed without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

2.3 "**Affiliate**" means an entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control with a party.

## 3. Obligations of the Recipient

The Recipient will:

3.1 use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

3.2 keep it confidential and protect it using at least the care it uses for its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care;

3.3 disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors, professional advisers and (with the Discloser's prior written consent) other persons who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by written or professional confidentiality duties at least as protective as this Agreement, and remain responsible for their compliance;

3.4 not copy it beyond what the Purpose requires, nor reverse engineer, decompile or attempt to derive source code, models or underlying data from any materials provided;

3.5 not input Confidential Information into any public or third-party AI service, or use it to train or improve any model, unless the Discloser has agreed in writing; and

3.6 notify the Discloser promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised access, use or disclosure, and cooperate to limit its effects.

## 4. Compelled disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it will (where lawful) give the Discloser prompt prior written notice so that it may seek a protective order, disclose only the portion legally required, and use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment.

## 5. Return and deletion

On the Discloser's written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will within {{15}} days return or securely delete all Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain: (a) copies required by law or its bona fide compliance policies; and (b) copies in routine electronic backups that are not readily accessible, provided they stay subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained and are not accessed.

## 6. Access to systems and code

6.1 If the Discloser grants access to repositories, environments or data, access is limited to what the Purpose requires and for the period agreed. The Recipient will not attempt to access anything beyond what has been granted.

6.2 The Discloser should not provide production personal data, payment-card data or health data under this Agreement. If it does, the Recipient may require that the data be removed and will handle it under applicable data-protection law until it is.

6.3 The Recipient will keep credentials in a secure manager, will not share them, and will notify the Discloser when access is no longer needed. The Discloser will revoke access at the end of the Purpose.

6.4 Security testing or scanning of the Discloser's systems requires the Discloser's prior written authorisation.

## 7. Ownership and no licence

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence or right is granted other than the limited right to use it for the Purpose.

7.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to disclose any information or to enter into any further agreement. No agreement on a business relationship will exist unless and until a definitive written agreement is signed.

7.3 **Feedback and residuals.** Each party remains free to use general skills, experience and know-how retained in unaided memory, provided this does not involve use or disclosure of the other's Confidential Information or infringe its intellectual property rights.

7.4 **Prior work.** Each party acknowledges the other may already have or be developing similar solutions. Nothing restricts either party from doing so independently, provided it does not use the other's Confidential Information.

## 8. No warranty

All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Discloser gives no warranty about its accuracy or completeness, and will have no liability arising from its use, except as set out in a definitive agreement.

## 9. Remedies

The parties agree that unauthorised use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which damages alone may be inadequate, and that the Discloser may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to other remedies, without proof of actual damages.

## 10. Term and survival

10.1 This Agreement applies to disclosures made during the period starting on the Effective Date and ending {{two}} years later, unless terminated earlier by either party on 30 days' written notice.

10.2 The Recipient's obligations continue for {{five}} years after the end of that disclosure period, except that for trade secrets and source code they continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret or is not public, and for personal data they continue as required by law.

10.3 If the parties enter into a Master Services Agreement or Statement of Work, the confidentiality terms in it will govern Confidential Information disclosed from the date it is signed, and this Agreement continues to govern information disclosed before then, unless that agreement states otherwise.

## 11. General

11.1 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of {{JURISDICTION}} and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of {{COURTS}}, except that either party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

11.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business who agrees to be bound.

11.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions. It may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

11.4 **No waiver; severability.** A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver. If a provision is unenforceable, the rest continues, and the provision is modified to the minimum extent needed.

11.5 **Export and compliance.** Each party will comply with applicable export-control, sanctions and anti-bribery laws in dealing with Confidential Information.

11.6 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above (or as updated by notice) by courier or email with confirmation of receipt.

11.7 **Counterparts and e-signature.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically, each of which is an original.

11.8 **Authority.** Each signatory confirms they are authorised to bind their party.

## Drafting notes (delete before sending)

- **Mutual vs one-way.** This is mutual because most prospects will share code, data and business plans, and Northbound will share methods and pricing. If a prospect insists on one-way, make sure your own tooling, pricing and methods remain protected another way.
- **Terms to confirm:** disclosure period (two years), tail (five years), return period, governing law and forum. Align with the MSA (`MSA.md` section 7, five-year tail; trade secrets and client data protected as long as they remain confidential).
- **AI clause (3.5).** Prospects may expect you to use AI coding tools on their code. State clearly in the MSA or SOW which tools and configurations are approved (for example Azure OpenAI in the client's tenant, with no training on client data) so this NDA is not breached by normal working practice.
- **Regulated clients** may require their own form of NDA. Compare against this one for term, residuals, and compelled-disclosure language.
- **Individuals.** If you share information with contractors, make sure they are bound by confidentiality obligations equivalent to section 3.3.
- **Employment and whistleblowing.** If this is adapted for employees or contractors, add the statutory carve-outs for protected disclosures that apply in your jurisdiction.
